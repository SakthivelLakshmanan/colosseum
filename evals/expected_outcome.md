# Expected outcome for DEC-001

**Question:** Should Legion Capital move 40% of its payment volume (about ₹600 crore a month) from Arvanta Payments to Brevis Pay?

Conditional, phased move. Do not move 40% now. Phase 1: move about 15% of volume to Brevis Pay (excluding Coral Bank and Tamra Bank traffic and the eNACH banks Brevis can't serve), only after Brevis (1) contractually commits to India-only processing and switches on its India-only fraud-scoring add-on, (2) closes the open high-severity pen test finding, (3) provides a current SOC 2 Type II report, and (4) accepts contract fixes: Indian governing law, audit rights for Legion and RBI, a 24-month price lock, a signed DPA and 2-hour incident notification. Phase 1 keeps Arvanta at 85%, so no exit fee. Serve 90 days' notice to Arvanta now. Phase 2: scale to 40% after a 60-day review (target before 31 Mar 2027 to meet the 70% vendor OKR and the board's cross-border deadline), accepting the ₹25 lakh exit fee, which the net monthly benefit pays back with the migration cost in about 6 months. Dissent to record: Legal (weaker Brevis contract terms) and InfoSec (vendor maturity, slow 2025 breach disclosure) favour waiting; Business favours moving faster.
